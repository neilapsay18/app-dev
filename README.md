	**	# My favorite movies	**
     ## AMERICAN SNIPER

     ** INFORMATION**
     -American Sniper is a 2014 war drama film directed by Clint Eastwood.
     -The movie stars Bradley Cooper as Chris Kyle, a U.S. Navy SEAL sniper.
     -It is based on Chris Kyle’s autobiography of the same name
     -The film shows Kyle’s experiences in Iraq and his struggles after returning home.
     -American Sniper received several Oscar nominations and was praised for its realism.
